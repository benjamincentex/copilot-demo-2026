Texas Education Freedom Accounts

What TEFA means for homeschool families

Texas launched its education savings account program for the 2026–27 school year. Here are the basics, in plain terms.

$2,000

per year for each homeschooled student, a fixed amount regardless of grade. Students with disabilities can qualify for more.

  • Funds can go to curriculum, tutoring, classes, therapies, technology and more.
  • You can only pay providers that are registered and approved by the program.
  • Homeschool parents cannot pay themselves "tuition" from the account.

Before you spend TEFA funds

  • Check that the tutor, class or program is approved using the state's provider finder.
  • Ask the provider whether they accept payment through the TEFA family portal.
  • Sign up for our monthly newsletter for a reminder when the next application window opens.

Rules and amounts can change. Always confirm on the official TEFA site before you spend.

Where things stand: the 2026–27 application window closed March 31, 2026. No date has been announced for 2027–28 yet. Join the state's interest list to hear when it opens. Join the 2027–28 interest list ↗
