Texas at a Glance

Six flags, nine eras, one amazing story.

Timeline

  • 13,000+ years ago · First TexansPeople camp at the Gault site near Georgetown. Later the Caddo, Tonkawa, Karankawa, Comanche and others.
  • 1519–1541 · Spanish explorersPineda maps the coast; Cabeza de Vaca shipwrecks near Galveston; Coronado crosses the Panhandle.
  • 1685–1731 · MissionsLa Salle's French fort, then Spanish missions. San Antonio and the Alamo founded in 1718.
  • 1821 · Mexican TexasMexico wins independence. Stephen F. Austin brings the Old Three Hundred.
  • 1835–1836 · RevolutionGonzales, the Declaration (Mar 2), the Alamo (Mar 6), Goliad, and victory at San Jacinto (Apr 21).
  • 1836–1845 · Republic of TexasPresident Sam Houston. Austin becomes the capital in 1839.
  • 1845–1870 · Statehood & Civil War28th state in 1845. Juneteenth in Galveston, June 19, 1865.
  • 1867–1900 · Cattle & railsChisholm Trail cattle drives, railroads, the 1888 Capitol, the 1900 Galveston storm.
  • 1901–today · Oil, space & growthSpindletop, CCC parks, President LBJ, Mission Control in Houston.

Texas symbols

State flower: bluebonnet · State tree: pecan · State bird: mockingbird · Motto: Friendship · Nickname: the Lone Star State

Days to celebrate

Oct 2 Come and Take It (Gonzales) · Mar 2 Texas Independence Day · Mar 6 Alamo Heroes Day · Apr 21 San Jacinto Day · Jun 19 Juneteenth · Dec 29 Statehood Day · March is Texas History Month
