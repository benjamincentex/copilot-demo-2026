Plan ahead: spring registration

Many co-ops open registration in the spring for the next school year, and the popular ones fill quickly. Two things help:

  • Tap "Notify me when spots open" on any group you like. We'll email you the moment they post openings or a registration date.
  • Check the "Registration opening soon" section on the site. It fills in as group leaders post their dates.

TEFA reminder

If your family has a Texas Education Freedom Account this year, homeschool students get $2,000 for curriculum, classes, tutoring and more, but only providers approved by the program can be paid. Check a provider's status on the Texas Comptroller's TEFA site before you buy. We'll share news about the next application window as soon as it's announced.
